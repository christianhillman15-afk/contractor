01 — About

A family tradition of quality, on-time construction.

Hamasaki Construction LLC is a fully insured and bondable general contracting firm licensed in the State of Hawaiʻi. Reorganized in 2016, HCLLC continues the family tradition of quality on-time construction — from resort renovations in Waikīkī to custom homes on Hawaiʻi Loa Ridge.

We are an SBA 8(a) certified firm and a City & County of Honolulu certified Erosion & Sediment Control Plan development and coordination company. Whatever the project — commercial or residential, new build or renovation — we manage it to meet your schedule and your budget.

  • SBA 8(a) certified firm
  • Fully insured and bondable
  • ESCP certified development & coordination
  • Statewide operations across Hawaiʻi

Erosion & Sediment Control Planning

ESCP approval, from plan to close-out.

City & County of Honolulu building permits require an approved Erosion & Sediment Control Plan before a permit is issued. Our certified ESCP Coordinator develops, coordinates, executes and monitors your ESCP for the project’s duration — through final inspection and permit close-out.

Requirements are determined by the Department of Planning and Permitting’s plan check and review process, and may vary by project.
